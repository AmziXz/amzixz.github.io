About

I'm AmziXz.

On YouTube I mod phone software — custom ROMs, root, debloating, and generally pushing devices past what they shipped with. I'm a fourth-year computer systems student, which is the reason the tutorials work instead of bricking your phone.

On TikTok I make car content and stream live. Unedited, on the move, whatever's actually happening. Everything gets shot on my phone with a DJI Osmo Mobile 8 gimbal and cut in DaVinci Resolve.

Off camera I run the social media for Catch The Baltic, a car show at Spilve Airfield in Riga — the accounts, the posts, and the outreach to drivers in Latvian and English. The next one is 22 August 2026.

If you're here for one of those three things, you'll probably tolerate the other two.
