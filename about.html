About

I'm AmziXz.

Content creator from Latvia. I make things, I break things on purpose, and lately I've started building companies around the things I care about.

[ 01 ] On camera

What I make

On YouTube I mod phone software — custom ROMs, root, debloating, and generally pushing devices past what they shipped with. I do it because I'm into IT and electronics, and I run every step on my own hardware first — which is why the tutorials work instead of bricking your phone.

On TikTok I make car content and stream live. Unedited, on the move, whatever's actually happening. Everything gets shot on my phone with a DJI Osmo Mobile 8 gimbal and cut in DaVinci Resolve.

[ 02 ] Off camera

What I'm building

I run the social media for Catch The Baltic — an organisation and registered business that puts on auto and moto events. That means the accounts, the posts, the announcement Telegram group, the organisation groups, and the outreach to drivers in Latvian and English.

I also co-own Blue Eye Energy, an energy drink company I'm building with a friend. It isn't on shelves yet — we're still assembling the pieces, and we're currently raising.

Longer term I want to build in the places I already spend my time: car tuning, parts, reengineered classic components that outlast the originals, and logistics. Those are plans rather than businesses today, and the ventures page is honest about which is which.
