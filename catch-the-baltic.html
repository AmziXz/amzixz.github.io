Catch The Baltic

An auto and moto event like nothing seen here before. Cars worth looking at, DJ sets, competitions, hosts on the mic, and plenty for both adults and kids.

Date 22 August 2026
Time 14:00 – 20:00
Where Spilve Airfield, Riga

Tickets

  • Expo participants Free
  • Children up to 12 (inclusive) Free
  • Visitors with reduced mobility Free
  • Ages 13 and up, and adults €10
  • Club members (20% off) €8
  • Lottery ticket €1

Cash only. Bring notes — there is no card payment on the gate.

Getting into the expo zone

Got a vehicle worth showing? The expo zone is free to enter, but you need to be approved first. Three steps:

  1. Follow the event on Instagram or TikTok.
  2. Send a DM with a photo of your vehicle.
  3. Include your name, phone number, registration plate, and the model.
