{%- comment -%} The variables every layout starts with. Included at the top of each layout, because Jekyll does not pass a layout's variables on to the layout around it. t the wording for this page's language (_data/lang/.yml) k every outside link (_data/links.yml) here this language's entry in _data/languages.yml home this language's home page: / or /lv/ v the cache-buster for /assets {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign t = site.data.lang[page.lang] -%} {%- assign k = site.data.links -%} {%- assign langs = site.data.languages -%} {%- for l in langs -%}{%- if l.code == page.lang -%}{%- assign here = l -%}{%- endif -%}{%- endfor -%} {%- assign home = here.prefix | append: "/" -%} {%- assign v = site.asset_version -%}
